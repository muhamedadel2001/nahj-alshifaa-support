Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Nahj Alshifaa is operated by Nahj Al-Shifaa General Medical Complex, Saudi Arabia ("we"). This policy explains how the app handles your information.

Information we collect

  • Account details such as your name, email and phone number.
  • Health information you use in the app, such as appointments, lab and radiology results, prescriptions and medical leave documents.
  • Your location, only when you choose an address for a home service.
  • Camera and microphone access, only during online consultations.
  • Payment is completed through Apple Pay or card. We do not store your full card details.
  • Push notification tokens, used to send appointment reminders.

How we use it

We use your information to provide consultations, home services, medical records, appointments, payments and reminders. We do not sell your personal data.

Sharing

We share data only with your doctors and care providers, and with service providers needed to run the app, such as payment, video call and notification services.

Your choices

You can delete your account and data at any time from Profile > Delete Account in the app. You can also contact us below.
